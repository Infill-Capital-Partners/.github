### About

**Infill Capital Partners** is a technology-driven real estate investment firm specialising in the **adaptive reuse** of underperforming urban assets across Europe. We convert obsolete commercial buildings into high-yield lifestyle hospitality venues — merging Japanese spatial efficiency with Italian design sensibility and data-driven operations.

Our flagship platform, **[Lume Hotels](https://lumehotels.com)**, redefines urban sleep through modular capsule architecture, circadian wellness systems, and institutional-grade operational standards.
