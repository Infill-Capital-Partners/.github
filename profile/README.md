### Overview

**Infill Capital Partners** is a vertically integrated European real estate investment platform executing **commercial-to-hospitality adaptive reuse** strategies across London, Paris, Milan, and Barcelona.

We identify and acquire structurally obsolete urban commercial assets — office buildings, retail blocks, and mixed-use properties — at significant discounts to replacement cost, and convert them into institutional-grade lifestyle micro-hotel assets under our proprietary **[Lume Hotels](https://lumehotels.com)** operating platform.

Our competitive advantage is **vertical integration**: acquisitions, design, development management, operations, and technology sit within a single platform, governed by the 270-page Lume Standards specification. This eliminates third-party execution risk, compresses conversion timelines to **4–6 months from site-to-open**, and enables consistent, predictable underwriting at scale.

We are actively building a scalable, multi-asset platform capable of deploying **€500M+ of equity** across the European urban hospitality conversion opportunity.

---

### Market Opportunity

Europe is sitting on a structural oversupply of obsolete commercial real estate — and a structural undersupply of quality, value-led urban accommodation.

**The Conversion Opportunity**
Post-pandemic, an estimated €2 trillion of European office, retail, and mixed-use stock is chronically underperforming. Vacancy rates in secondary urban commercial assets remain elevated across all four of our target markets, with many owners seeking exit at deep discounts to book value. This creates a generational acquisition window for disciplined, execution-capable buyers.

**The Hospitality Demand Gap**
Urban European hospitality has recovered strongly post-COVID. ADR and RevPAR in gateway city markets have reached historic highs, yet the supply pipeline for quality-branded budget-to-midscale accommodation remains constrained. Modular capsule hotels offer a disruptive value proposition — premium experience at accessible price points — targeting the fastest-growing segment of urban travellers.

**The Adaptive Reuse Arbitrage**
Buying at distressed commercial values and converting to stabilised hotel assets creates significant embedded equity before any operational value is captured. Our conversion cost model — benchmarked across live deals — consistently underwrites to purchase + conversion + FF&E well below the stabilised hotel valuation on a per-key basis in our target markets.

**Target Addressable Market** — Estimated 10,000+ eligible conversion assets across London, Paris, Milan, and Barcelona alone; representing a multi-decade, multi-cycle pipeline opportunity.

**Target Assets** — 500–5,000 sq m urban core commercial buildings; office, retail, and mixed-use; freehold and long leasehold; planning potential for C1 (hotel) or mixed-use change of use

**Markets** — London · Paris · Milan · Barcelona (gateway cities with deep distressed stock, liquid hotel investment markets, and strong structural tourism demand)

**Return Framework** — Targeting IRR in the high-teens to low-twenties; 30%+ RevPAR premium over traditional hostel comp sets; stabilised yield-on-cost materially above prime hotel cap rates in each market

**Asset Profile** — 50–200 key micro-hotel conversions; mixed-use ground floor activations (F&B, wellness, co-working) where planning supports; bolt-on portfolio aggregation strategy targeting 20+ assets and 2,000+ keys at platform scale

### Risk-Adjusted Return Profile

Infill Capital Partners is designed to deliver institutional-quality risk-adjusted returns through structural downside protection at every stage:

**Acquisition** — Assets acquired at 30–50% discount to replacement cost; embedded equity created before conversion commences; no speculative development land risk

**Conversion** — Modular pod system with fixed-price procurement, standardised MEP and FF&E packages, and an in-house development management function limits cost overrun risk; 4–6 month site-to-open timeline minimises carry cost and interest drag

**Operations** — Lume Hotels operating platform delivers consistent RevPAR performance from a lean, technology-enabled team; AI-driven revenue management and cost control reduce OpEx-to-Revenue ratio versus traditional hotel management models

**Downside** — Hotel assets with established trading history in urban gateway markets are liquid; in a stress scenario, assets retain residential, student accommodation, or serviced apartment value — providing a hard-asset floor not available in development-only strategies

### ESG & Sustainable Investment

Adaptive reuse is inherently the most sustainable form of real estate investment. Our strategy delivers measurable ESG outperformance versus ground-up development on every material dimension.

**Carbon** — Retaining the existing building structure and envelope eliminates the embodied carbon cost of demolition and new build. Life-cycle carbon analysis consistently shows 40–60% embodied carbon reduction versus equivalent new-build hotel development. We target BREEAM Very Good or equivalent on all converted assets and document carbon performance for LP reporting.

**Urban Regeneration** — Our acquisitions target economically inactive or blighted urban assets, often in transitional neighbourhoods. Conversion to active hospitality use increases local economic activity, improves streetscape, and supports city-level objectives for commercial-to-residential/mixed-use zone densification that municipal planning authorities across all four markets are actively incentivising.

**Social Value** — Lume Hotels are designed to democratise access to high-quality, wellness-led urban accommodation. Our price positioning serves business and leisure travellers who have historically been underserved by a market that forces a choice between luxury hotels and poor-quality budget accommodation.

**Governance** — We operate to institutional governance standards from day one: independent advisory structures, AIFMD-compatible fund and SPV frameworks, LP-grade reporting, and ESG KPI dashboards maintained alongside financial performance reporting.

We are positioned to achieve GRESB participation at the portfolio level as the platform scales, providing third-party validated ESG benchmarking for institutional LP requirements.

### Operating Model

Infill Capital Partners operates as a fully integrated real estate platform across four functions:

**1. Acquisitions & Underwriting**
Sourcing, screening, and structuring commercial-to-hospitality conversion opportunities. Proprietary underwriting models incorporate local RevPAR benchmarks, conversion cost databases, and AI-assisted comparable analysis. Off-market deal origination through direct owner outreach and broker relationships in each target market. Target: 100+ assets screened per quarter; 2–4 acquisitions per year at platform scale.

**2. Design & Development Management**
In-house architectural direction and development management, executing conversions to the Lume Standards specification. Modular pod systems and standardised MEP and FF&E packages deliver repeatable cost and programme certainty. Conversion timeline: 4–6 months site-to-open. Cost certainty: fixed-price procurement via pre-qualified supply chain across all four markets.

**3. Hospitality Operations**
Operational management of live assets under the Lume Hotels brand. Revenue management, guest experience, digital check-in, housekeeping, and F&B managed through integrated technology systems. One central operations team capable of managing 10+ properties. RevPAR performance benchmarked monthly against local comp sets.

**4. Technology & Asset Intelligence**
Internal engineering maintains the digital infrastructure underpinning deal origination, portfolio management, investor reporting, and guest experience. All systems are proprietary, not third-party SaaS dependent, ensuring data sovereignty and platform continuity for institutional LPs.

### AI Agents in Real Estate Operations

We have re-architected the firm's entire workflow around a three-layer AI agent stack — one of the most advanced operational AI deployments in European hospitality real estate.

**Directive Layer** — Investment criteria, underwriting logic, brand standards, and operating thresholds defined by senior leadership become the governing rules for the AI systems below. Human judgement sets the guardrails; AI executes within them.

**Orchestration Layer** — AI agents coordinate complex, multi-step workflows across functions:
- Automated deal screening and preliminary underwriting from commercial property data feeds
- Due diligence workflow coordination — tracking tasks, surfacing gaps, flagging timeline risks
- Portfolio KPI monitoring with exception-based alerts to asset managers
- Investor reporting drafts generated from live financial and operational data
- Design specification review against Lume Standards, flagging non-compliant submissions

**Execution Layer** — Task-level agents handle high-volume, repeatable work: market data aggregation, RevPAR benchmarking, compliance checklist processing, guest communication, and financial reconciliations.

For institutional capital partners, this architecture translates directly to **better underwriting accuracy**, **lower operational overhead per key**, **faster reporting cycles**, and **stronger portfolio governance** than firms operating on traditional staffing models — directly improving net returns without compromising institutional-grade oversight.

The **Lume Standards Guide** (v6.2.0) is a 270+ page proprietary specification — the institutional backbone of the platform. It codifies every design, engineering, and operational decision across the portfolio, enabling consistent quality, consistent underwriting, and consistent LP reporting at scale.

- **Acoustic Engineering** — STC 55+ rated, 7-layer decoupled pod assemblies; sleep-grade sound isolation benchmarked against five-star hotel standards
- **Sleep Technology** — Circadian lighting protocols and 7.83 Hz Schumann Resonance integration; measurable guest sleep quality outcomes driving repeat booking and NPS
- **Modular Architecture** — WeCapsule Gen 2 pods; 4–6 month site-to-open conversion at fixed, repeatable cost; enables accurate underwriting across all markets
- **Digital Guest Journey** — NFC Smart Wallet check-in, in-app controls, and seamless departure via SALTO Space; near-zero front desk staffing requirement
- **Wellness Infrastructure** — Spa-grade bathrooms, Finnish sauna, cold plunge, and biohacking amenities standard across all assets; wellness positioning captures RevPAR premium at every price tier

The Lume Standard is what separates our assets from budget hospitality, undifferentiated serviced apartments, and standard hostel conversion products — and it is the specification against which we underwrite consistent RevPAR and yield-on-cost performance across the portfolio.
