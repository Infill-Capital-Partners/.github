### About

**Infill Capital Partners** is a European real estate investment firm specialising in the **adaptive reuse** of underperforming urban commercial assets. We acquire obsolete post-COVID office, retail, and mixed-use properties and convert them into high-yield lifestyle micro-hotels — targeting 30%+ RevPAR premiums over conventional hospitality through superior design, operational efficiency, and proprietary technology.

Our flagship hospitality platform, **[Lume Hotels](https://lumehotels.com)**, delivers institutional-grade capsule hotels that combine modular architecture, circadian wellness systems, and AI-powered operations — purpose-built for the urban traveller seeking sleep quality, privacy, and wellness at accessible price points.

We are an operationally integrated investor, meaning design, development, technology, and asset management sit under one roof. This vertical integration compresses conversion timelines to **4–6 months from site-to-open**, reduces third-party risk, and produces assets to a repeatable, scalable standard.

### Operating Model

Infill Capital Partners operates as a fully integrated real estate platform across four functions:

**1. Acquisitions & Underwriting**
Sourcing, screening, and structuring commercial-to-hospitality conversion opportunities. Our proprietary underwriting models incorporate local RevPAR benchmarks, conversion cost databases, and AI-assisted comparable analysis to produce rapid, reliable deal decisions.

**2. Design & Development Management**
In-house architectural direction and development management, executing conversions to the Lume Standards specification. Modular pod systems and standardised MEP and FF&E packages compress programme and cost.

**3. Hospitality Operations**
Operational management of live assets under the Lume Hotels brand. Revenue management, guest experience, digital check-in, housekeeping, and F&B are all managed through integrated technology systems, enabling one central team to run multiple properties.

**4. Technology & Asset Intelligence**
Internal engineering builds and maintains the digital infrastructure underpinning deal origination, portfolio management, investor reporting, and guest experience — ensuring every decision is data-driven.

### AI Agents in Real Estate Operations

We are building one of the most advanced AI operations layers in European hospitality real estate. Our position is simple: **AI agents do not replace our team — they multiply what our team can do.**

Rather than bolt AI tools onto a traditional operating model, we have re-architected the firm's workflow around a three-layer AI agent stack:

**Directive Layer** — Senior leadership defines investment criteria, underwriting logic, brand standards, and operating thresholds. These become the governing rules for the AI systems below.

**Orchestration Layer** — AI agents coordinate complex, multi-step workflows across functions. Examples include:
- Automated deal screening and preliminary underwriting from sourcing data feeds
- Due diligence workflow coordination — tracking tasks, surfacing missing items, flagging timeline risks
- Portfolio KPI monitoring with exception-based alerts to asset managers
- Investor reporting drafts generated from live financial and operational data
- Design specification review against the Lume Standards, flagging non-compliant submissions

**Execution Layer** — Task-level agents and integrations handle high-volume, repeatable work: market data aggregation, RevPAR benchmarking, compliance checklist processing, guest communication, and financial reconciliations.

This architecture means a compact team of experienced operators, investors, and designers can manage an institutional-grade portfolio — with AI agents handling throughput, standardisation, and data quality at a scale that would otherwise require a much larger headcount.

For capital partners, this translates directly to **better underwriting accuracy**, **lower operational overhead**, **faster reporting cycles**, and **stronger portfolio governance** than firms operating on traditional staffing models.

### The Lume Standard

The **Lume Standards Guide** (v6.2.0) is a 270+ page proprietary specification codifying every dimension of design, engineering, and operations across the Lume Hotels portfolio. It is our single most important tool for maintaining quality, consistency, and underwriting reliability at scale.

- **Acoustic Engineering** — STC 55+ rated, 7-layer decoupled pod assemblies ensuring sleep-grade sound isolation
- **Sleep Technology** — Circadian lighting protocols and 7.83 Hz Schumann Resonance integration for restorative sleep environments
- **Modular Architecture** — WeCapsule Gen 2 pods enabling 4–6 month site-to-open conversion at repeatable cost
- **Digital Guest Journey** — NFC Smart Wallet check-in, in-app controls, and seamless departure via SALTO Space integration
- **Wellness Infrastructure** — Spa-grade bathrooms, Finnish sauna, cold plunge, and biohacking amenities standard across all assets

The Lume Standard is what separates our assets from both traditional budget hospitality and undifferentiated serviced apartment products — and it is the basis on which we underwrite consistent RevPAR outperformance.
